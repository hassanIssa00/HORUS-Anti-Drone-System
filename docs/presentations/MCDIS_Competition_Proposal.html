1. Executive Summary

MCDIS (Multi-Modal Counter-Drone Intelligence System) is a fully functional AI software backbone designed to serve as the central intelligence layer of any Counter-Unmanned Aerial System (C-UAS) defense network.

Unlike hardware-only solutions, MCDIS focuses on the brain of the defense system — combining deep learning computer vision (YOLOv8), multi-sensor fusion, kinematic threat analysis, and an automated countermeasure decision engine into a single, deployable software platform.

Key Innovation: MCDIS introduces a Kinematic Discrimination Engine that classifies airborne targets as birds, commercial drones, or high-speed missiles based on real-time trajectory analysis, speed profiling, and object geometry — dramatically reducing false positive rates compared to pure visual-only detection systems.

2. Problem Statement

The proliferation of low-cost commercial UAVs has created an asymmetric threat landscape. Drones can be weaponized for reconnaissance, payload delivery, and even kamikaze attacks using fiber-optic guidance that defeats traditional electronic warfare.

Critical challenges addressed by MCDIS:

  • Detection at range: Small drones (DJI Mini: 4cm² RCS) are nearly invisible to conventional radar
  • Bird vs. Drone discrimination: Up to 40% of alerts in existing systems are false positives from birds
  • Multi-threat response: Different drone types require different countermeasures (jamming vs. kinetic)
  • 24/7 operation: Night and low-visibility conditions degrade optical detection
  • Fiber-optic drones: Modern attack drones bypass all RF jamming — requiring kinetic-only engagement

5.3 Radar System (Simulated)

A physics-based radar simulation engine with real-world calibrated parameters:

  • Micro-Doppler classification: Distinguishes rotor harmonics from wing-flap signatures
  • Three-ring threat zones: OUTER (5km) → MIDDLE (2km) → INNER (500m) with escalating alerts
  • Track management: Nearest-neighbor association with automatic track initiation and pruning
  • Real-world RCS database: Calibrated cross-sections for 12 drone types (0.001–5.0 m²)

10. Conclusion

MCDIS represents a complete AI-driven C-UAS software backbone — a deployable intelligence layer that transforms raw sensor data into actionable threat intelligence and autonomous defense decisions.

The system's modular, open architecture means it can be integrated with any existing hardware infrastructure — from a single PTZ camera to a distributed multi-node radar network — without requiring proprietary vendor lock-in.

With its working detection pipeline, persistent tracking, kinematic discrimination, and tactical dashboard already proven in testing, MCDIS is positioned as a practical, innovative, and scalable solution to the growing drone threat.
